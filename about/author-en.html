I created this school as a place to study artificial intelligence step by step — from the foundations of machine learning to the inner mechanisms of neural networks and the engineering of AI systems.

Learning should be more than using ready-made tools. We explore how models work, what their answers are based on, how explanations can be evaluated, and how knowledge can become practical systems.

AREAS OF INTEREST

Three areas of focus

These fields form the educational architecture of AI Research DCH School.

01 · UNDERSTAND

Explainable AI

Explainable AI, model interpretability, evaluation of explanations, and understanding the limits of algorithmic predictions.

02 · RESEARCH

Neural network research

Mechanistic interpretability, internal representations, circuits, and the study of how models produce their outputs.

03 · BUILD

Practical AI engineering

Local language models, RAG, integrations, tools, and the development of applied AI systems.

WHY THIS SCHOOL

Understand.
Evaluate.
Build.

AI Research DCH School is built around a research mindset: learn the foundations, formulate hypotheses, run experiments, evaluate results, and be transparent about limitations. That is why the courses combine theory, hands-on tasks, visual explanations, and knowledge checks. The goal is to help learners move beyond surface-level AI usage towards informed, independent work with these technologies.
