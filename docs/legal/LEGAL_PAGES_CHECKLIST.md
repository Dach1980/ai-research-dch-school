# Legal pages required before public registration

Registration must remain disabled until the school owner has approved final Terms of Service and Privacy Policy. The documents must identify the actual data controller/operator, contact details, applicable jurisdiction, processing purposes and legal bases, Supabase region and subprocessors, retention periods, user rights, deletion/contact procedures, payment/refund terms (before payments), and effective dates. This checklist is not legal advice and is not a substitute for review by qualified counsel.
