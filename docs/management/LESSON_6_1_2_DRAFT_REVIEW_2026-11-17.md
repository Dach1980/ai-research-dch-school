## Learning design

The lesson builds on 6.1.1 and clarifies the operational distinction used in this course:

- **Interpretability / interpretable-by-design:** understanding the model through its own representation, structure, parameters, and computations.
- **Explainability / post-hoc explainability:** producing an account of an already-trained model's behaviour through an additional analysis procedure.
- **Explanation:** a concrete answer or representation addressing a defined question about a model or output.

The lesson explicitly notes that research terminology is not universally standardized. It compares direct model inspection with feature attribution, local surrogates, counterfactual explanations, and global post-hoc analysis; discusses faithfulness, stability, scope, audience comprehension, and reproducibility; and warns against treating feature attribution as causal evidence.
