Introducing AI Research DCH School

A first look at the platform and its initial learning tracks.

3 November 2026

The initial announcement introduced three tracks: Understanding AI, Inside AI and Building with AI. This is a historical record of the first introduction; the current curriculum contains four tracks. AI Research DCH School — open beta

We began gradually opening AI Research DCH School, an educational project focused on studying AI, researching modern neural networks and building practical AI systems.

Our goal was sequential learning: from foundational concepts and mathematics to neural network internals and building AI applications.

Three learning tracks

Track A — Understanding AI: the basics of AI, machine learning, model evaluation and explanation methods.

Track B — Inside AI: neural network architectures, internal representations, response mechanisms and mechanistic interpretability.

Track C — Building with AI: local language models, APIs, RAG, tools and building AI applications.

The first lecture

The first lecture A.1 covered the definition of AI, its history, approaches, task types, and limitations. It included explanations of terms, self-check questions and practice.

What comes next

New lectures, practical materials and original notes on technologies and research will appear as they are prepared and checked. We prioritise quality over quantity. This is the original first-look announcement.
