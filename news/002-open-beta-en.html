A school we build together

I invite you to explore my educational project dedicated to studying, researching and applying artificial intelligence. Our aim is to connect foundational knowledge, understanding how models work, and building AI systems independently.

What is available now

At the open-beta launch, 53 of 60 lessons in the first track, XAI Practitioner, have been published. Materials include theory, formulas, practical exercises, illustrations, glossaries and knowledge checks. Lessons are available in Russian, English and Chinese. The full curriculum plans 167 lessons across four tracks; the remaining tracks will be developed gradually.

Open beta and free access

The site is under active development. Access to learning materials is currently free. The interface and content may change, and temporary technical issues are possible. We continue to validate and improve the material and will gradually publish new lessons, labs and research notes.

Help develop the school

Let's build this school together! I welcome comments, ideas and suggestions on lesson content, the curriculum, interface and practical exercises. Researchers, engineers, educators and anyone interested in AI are invited to collaborate.

Infrastructure and hosting in Russia

I would also appreciate recommendations for reliable Russian hosting and ways to deploy the educational platform. Stable availability, SSL, automated deployment, backups and room to grow are important. No decision to migrate the site has been made yet.
